### confidence-cascade
**What it does:** Decision confidence cascades with three-zone model (GREEN ≥0.90, YELLOW 0.75–0.89, RED <0.75). Sequential confidence multiplies; parallel confidence averages with weights.  
**What works:** 15 tests, published as npm package, dist built, full TypeScript implementation.  
**USE NOW:** Feed into any decision pipeline that needs confidence-weighted routing. Strategy Guild, model selection, safety gates.  
**KEY FILES:** `src/confidence-cascade.ts`, `tests/confidence-cascade.test.ts`  
**MATURITY:** High — npm package with dist, 15 tests.
